# goodwill-impairment-risk

## Question

A colleague says: 'Goodwill is 45% of total assets, so depreciation expense will be high in the next 12 months.' Assess that premise and predict the risk of a material goodwill-related earnings charge during the prediction horizon.

- 信息截止 / As of: 2025-03-31
- 预测区间 / Horizon: next 12 months
- 会计辖区 / Jurisdiction: IFRS

### 已知资料 / Evidence

- 观察日期 / Observed: 2025-03-20
- 来源 / Source: Synthetic FY2024 annual report

Reported goodwill is CU 4.5 billion, equal to 45% of total assets and 82% of book equity.

### accounting-policy

- 观察日期 / Observed: 2025-03-20
- 来源 / Source: Synthetic accounting policy note

The group applies IFRS. Goodwill is not amortised; it is tested annually and when impairment indicators exist under IAS 36.

### operating-trend

- 观察日期 / Observed: 2025-03-20
- 来源 / Source: Synthetic segment note

The cash-generating unit holding 76% of goodwill recorded a 12% revenue decline and a 430 basis point EBITDA-margin decline.

### headroom

- 观察日期 / Observed: 2025-03-20
- 来源 / Source: Synthetic impairment note

Management's latest impairment test showed recoverable amount only 6% above carrying amount for that cash-generating unit.

### sensitivity

- 观察日期 / Observed: 2025-03-20
- 来源 / Source: Synthetic sensitivity disclosure

A 50 basis point increase in the discount rate, with other assumptions unchanged, would eliminate the disclosed headroom.

### rates

- 观察日期 / Observed: 2025-03-31
- 来源 / Source: Synthetic PIT market snapshot

The relevant five-year market yield increased by 35 basis points after management's impairment-test date.

### dividend-policy

- 观察日期 / Observed: 2025-03-20
- 来源 / Source: Synthetic dividend announcement

The board retained the ordinary dividend at last year's per-share amount.
